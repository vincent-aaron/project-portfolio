"We built Otherworld because the isekai shelf deserved better curation than an algorithm."

Otherworld started as a spreadsheet of light novels three friends kept trading between them. Three years and a few hundred imports later, it's a full storefront — still stocked the same way: by people who finish the series before they recommend it.

We work directly with publishers and translators to bring English-language editions to readers who'd otherwise be waiting on fan scans.
